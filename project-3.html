1 · Overview

Project overview & problem statement

The problem

Telecom companies lose revenue every time a customer leaves, and it costs more to win a new customer than to keep one. The goal is to spot likely leavers early so the company can act before they go.

Main goals

  • Predict which customers are likely to churn.
  • Handle the imbalance between customers who stay and customers who leave.
  • Keep the model simple by dropping features that add nothing.

3 · Methodology

Data, process & methodology

1
Load and inspect
Loaded the telecom customer records (7,000+) and checked the share of customers who left.
2
Balance the classes
Only a minority of customers leave, so a model can look accurate by always guessing “stays”. SMOTE creates synthetic examples of the minority group so the model learns the leaving pattern.
3
Select features
Used correlation and importance scores to keep useful features and drop the rest.
4
Train the models
Trained XGBoost and Random Forest, two tree-based methods that work well on tabular data.
5
Evaluate
Measured ROC-AUC, which shows how well the model ranks leavers above stayers (50% is guessing, 100% is perfect).

5 · Conclusion

Conclusion & business impact

Conclusion

The model ranks customers by their risk of leaving, so retention effort can go to the highest-risk group first instead of everyone.

Actionable takeaways

  • A company can contact high-risk customers with an offer before they leave.
  • Fewer features make the model easier to explain and maintain.
  • Imbalanced data needs special handling, or a model looks good but misses the customers who matter.
